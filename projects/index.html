The whole project

Every project

WittyMediaServer is made of many projects, each with its own repository, versions and releases. Here they all are.

Apps

What you install.

WittyMediaServer0.7.2-alpha

Your films, shows, discs, music, books and games, on every screen in your home.

wittywilla/WittyMediaServer

WittyMediaClient0.4.1

Apps for TV boxes, phones, tablets and desktops that connect to your WittyMediaServer, for the devices where a browser isn’t the right fit.

wittywilla/WittyMediaClient

WittyMediaPlayer

A desktop player for discs and files, with real DVD and Blu-ray menus. Windows only for now.

wittywilla/WittyMediaPlayer

WittyReader0.1.1

A reader for books, PDFs and comics, with notes and a pen, on your own device.

wittywilla/WittyReader

Modules

Added to a server from its store, each a project of its own.

WittyBluray0.4.0

Blu-ray images with their menus, including 4K, HDR and 3D discs.

wittywilla/WittyBluray

WittyCD0.2.1

Audio CD images played as discs: gapless, with their tracks, indexes and CD-TEXT, in a CD player.

wittywilla/WittyCD

WittyComics0.3.0

Comics and manga: pages, spreads, vertical strips, either reading direction.

wittywilla/WittyComics

WittyCouch0.3.0

Play the games on any PC running Sunshine, in your browser.

wittywilla/WittyCouch

WittyCouch Host0.3.0

The separate AGPL-3.0 program (with GPL-3.0 Moonlight parts) that carries WittyCouch's game streams, built on Moonlight's code.

wittywilla/WittyCouchHost

WittyDVD0.4.0

DVD images with their real menus, chapters, angles and subtitles.

wittywilla/WittyDVD

WittyEbook0.3.0

Reflowable books (EPUB) with book view, typography and study tools.

wittywilla/WittyEbook

WittyInk0.3.0

What every reader shares: annotations, notes, definitions, citations and handwriting.

wittywilla/WittyInk

WittyIntegratedFileBrowser0.3.0

Choosing a server's folders from any device. Read-only.

wittywilla/WittyIntegratedFileBrowser

WittyPDF0.3.0

PDFs with selectable text, zoom, outline, thumbnails and search.

wittywilla/WittyPDF

WittyPhotos0.4.0

Photo libraries: what a photo says about itself, including RAW files, without decoding it.

wittywilla/WittyPhotos

WittyPlay0.4.1

Console games streamed to a browser or an app, with every controller and a touch layout editor.

wittywilla/WittyPlay

WittyRigs0.3.0

An advanced option: host demanding PC games on your server's own hardware.

wittywilla/WittyRigs

WittyStats0.2.0

A statistics screen you arrange yourself: the machine, streams, libraries and history, as charts or tables, with exports.

wittywilla/WittyStats

WittyTuner0.3.0

Live TV and recording: network tuners, IPTV playlists, capture cards and guide data. Encrypted channels are refused.

wittywilla/WittyTuner

WittyVisualizer0.3.0

Light shows that follow the music: spectrum bars, scopes, plasma, tunnels, starfields, feedback and more, full screen.

wittywilla/WittyVisualizer

The manifest format, tools and kit that modules are built with.

wittywilla/WittyModule

WittyModuleCatalog

The signed list of modules and versions that a server’s store reads. It holds no module code.

wittywilla/WittyModuleCatalog

WittyDisc

Reads DVD and Blu-ray images without mounting them. The disc modules are built on it.

wittywilla/WittyDisc

Alpha software: it changes quickly and can fail or lose data. Keep backups, including your data folder, before you upgrade. No media comes with WittyMediaServer. Copy-protected discs, encrypted broadcasts and encrypted game files are refused with a plain message. Nothing here unlocks them.

This site has no cookies, trackers or analytics. Your theme choice is kept in your own browser. How this site handles privacy. Change the theme.

WittyMediaServer and this website are open source under the Apache License 2.0. Every picture shows only openly licensed or public-domain media, each credited.
