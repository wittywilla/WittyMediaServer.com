0.3.0-alpha was never published, so 0.4.0-alpha is the first release with everything from it too.

**More than films.** There are new libraries for music, audiobooks, podcasts, books, comics and manga, and anime. Music plays in a bar that keeps going while you look around, and audiobooks pick up where you stopped on any device. Follow a podcast by searching a directory or pasting its feed. Books open in a reader: comics page by page or as a strip, EPUBs paged or scrolling, and PDFs in the browser. A book can't run scripts.

**Everyone at home.** A “Who's watching?” screen, and Kids and Guest profiles beside adult accounts. Each account can have an age level, the libraries it may open and genres it doesn't see, and the server keeps to them everywhere, from search to playback. A profile with no password can only sign in at home.

**Less to set up.** TV shows get their details from TVmaze, and films from Wikidata and Wikipedia, with no key. Posters for films still need a free TMDB key. A setup wizard walks you through the first account and your libraries, and a folder browser chooses the server's folders from any device.

**Updates in one click.** Settings shows the newest version with its notes, and **Update now** downloads it, checks it and restarts. Each release's list of files is signed with the project's key, and the server refuses anything that doesn't match. Your data is never touched, and a failed update puts every file back. On a Mac, in Docker and anywhere else the server can't replace its own files, it says so and links to the download. On Windows, a new setup program installs for your account without administrator rights.

**Discs.** DVDs with several angles play the one you choose, and remember your language, subtitles and angle for next time. A disc's page leads with the film and a picture of every scene, then its extras and menus. **Back** keeps a video playing in a corner.

**Easier for everyone.** Every theme meets WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, arrow keys and a remote move between controls on every page, game controllers work, and the player tells a screen reader what's happening.
