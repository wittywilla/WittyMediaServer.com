Copy this file to a new name that starts with the date, such as `2026-11-10-what-changed.md`.
The part after the date becomes the page's address: `/news/what-changed/`.

Then change the lines at the top:

- **title** and **summary**: what people see in lists.
- **date**: the day it's published, as year-month-day.
- **release**: the repository and tag it's about, or delete the line if it isn't about a release.
- **image**: a picture from content/shots.json, such as home, dvd-menu or wittytv-guide, or delete the line.
- **draft**: delete this line when it's ready. A draft isn't published.

Write the article here, below the second line of dashes. **Bold**, *italic*, [links](https://example.com),
lists and `code` all work. Plain and short, as in the server's own pages.
