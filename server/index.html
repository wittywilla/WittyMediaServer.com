The server

WittyMediaServer

Self-hosted software that keeps your films, shows, discs, music, books and games on a computer you own, and streams them to every screen in your home. It plays DVDs and Blu-rays with their real menus, in any browser.

0.7.1-alphaReleased 6 November 2026Apache-2.0
DownloadGetting started guideGitHub

Install it on the computer that holds your media. Every other screen reaches it through a web browser, with no app needed. Discs, games, photos, books and TV tuners are modules you add from the server’s store. The server works without any of them.

Films and TV

Your films and shows, looked after

Posters, plots, cast and ratings arrive with no API key, and you can add free keys or your own sources for more. Everything you watch picks up where you left off, on any screen.

  • Continue watching, skip intro and instant seeking
  • Collections, including smart ones that fill themselves
  • Details from files beside your media, or looked up for you
  • Converts video only when a device needs it

Discs

DVDs and Blu-rays, with their real menus

With the WittyDVD and WittyBluray modules, disc images and folders play like a disc player: the intro, the animated menu, scene selection, extras, and the disc’s own audio and subtitles. No mounting, no ripping to files first. Blu-rays have been tested on custom-made discs, so retail discs may show problems not seen yet.

  • DVD images and VIDEO_TS folders, with menus, chapters and angles
  • Blu-ray images and BDMV folders, including 4K and HDR
  • Audio CD images, played gapless in an on-screen CD player
  • Copy-protected discs are refused with a plain message. Nothing unlocks them

Live TV and channels

Channels made from your own library

WittyTV turns your libraries into channels in one press, with a guide that looks like real TV, and needs no extra hardware. Add the WittyTuner module for live TV and recording from network tuners and IPTV playlists. IPTV has been tested with local streams; tuners and capture cards haven’t been tried on real hardware yet.

  • Guide looks of its own: modern, classic, teletext and more
  • Network tuners, IPTV playlists and capture cards
  • Recording from tuner channels, with a list of what’s been recorded
  • Encrypted channels are refused, never decrypted

Music and audiobooks

Everything you listen to

Albums and artists, internet radio, podcasts you follow, and audiobooks that remember your place. Sing along with lyrics and karaoke, or watch WittyVisualizer’s light shows.

  • Albums, artists and playlists
  • Lyrics and karaoke
  • Podcasts, downloaded for you
  • Audiobooks that remember your place

Books and comics

A reader for every kind of book

Read EPUB books, PDFs, comics and manga in the browser, with highlights, notes, definitions and a pen. Your place follows you from screen to screen.

  • EPUB with book view and typography
  • PDFs with selectable text, outline and search
  • Comics and manga, in either reading direction
  • Highlights, notes and handwriting

Photos and videos

Photos, and the videos people make

A timeline of your photos with albums and an offline map, a feed of vertical videos you swipe through, and creators’ videos grouped by who made them.

  • A timeline, albums and an offline map
  • RAW previews and camera details
  • Shorts in a feed you swipe through
  • Creator videos, music videos and concerts by channel

Games

Your own games, played in a browser

WittyPlay runs your game files on the server and streams them to the page, so a phone or a TV can play them with a gamepad, keyboard or touch. WittyCouch plays games on a PC of your own that runs Sunshine. Neither has been tried with a real controller or a real gaming PC yet.

  • Games run on the server and stream to any screen
  • Gamepads, keyboards and touch controls
  • Games on your own PC through WittyCouch
  • You bring the games. None come with it

Everyone at home

One server, everyone’s own place

Each person gets their own progress, lists and settings.

Who’s watching?

Profiles with their own progress, favourites and game saves, each a tile to choose at home.

Kids profiles

Kids see only titles up to the age you choose. Titles with no rating stay hidden until you say otherwise.

Every screen

Phones, tablets, laptops and TVs with a web browser. The apps add more.

Modules

Start small, add what you want

Modules add what your server can do, such as DVD menus, streamed games or live TV. Most are projects of their own, signed, and added from Settings → Modules while the server keeps running.

  • Signed, and checked before they run
  • Added and updated without a restart
  • Removing one leaves your media and history as they are

Where it runs

Platforms and architectures

These are the files in 0.7.1-alpha. amd64 is most PCs; arm64 is Apple silicon and many small boards. Linux gets the most testing. The Windows installer has been tested under Wine, not on real Windows. The FreeBSD package has run in virtual machines, not on real hardware. The Mac installer has never been run.

Any architecture your host supports

FFmpeg comes with every download except FreeBSD on arm64, which needs pkg install ffmpeg. Phones, tablets and TVs need none of these: open the server’s address in a browser, or use WittyMediaClient.

Alpha software: it changes quickly and can fail or lose data. Keep backups, including your data folder, before you upgrade. No media comes with WittyMediaServer. Copy-protected discs, encrypted broadcasts and encrypted game files are refused with a plain message. Nothing here unlocks them. Legal notes.

Try it on a computer you already have

Install the server, open its page in a browser, and point it at a folder of films. Setup takes a few minutes.
