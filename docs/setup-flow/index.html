First-time setup: the flow

This page says what first-time setup asks, in what order, and why. It is the plan the setup pages (web/src/pages/setup/, SetupWizard.tsx, setup/plan.ts) and the server’s setup routes (internal/server/setup_state.go) follow. FIRST-TIME-SETUP.md is the guide for people setting up a server; this page is for whoever changes setup.

The rules#

  1. Six numbered steps: Welcome, Your account, Modules, Your media, Look, Finish. The count never changes under someone.
  2. Each page has a short plain title, one sentence at most, and one obvious main button. No “Skip for now” under the card, and no “You can change this later in Settings, …” on the app’s own pages: Welcome says once that everything can be changed later. A module’s own page may still say where its settings are.
  3. The usual answer is already chosen. Pressing the main button on every page gives a working server.
  4. Two ways through, chosen on Welcome and kept in this browser (wms.setupAdvanced) until setup ends:
    • Quick setup shows only what’s needed to start watching. Every other choice is behind the step’s Customize button (parts.tsx, Customize and Option): a dialog of folded parts, each with what it is in a few words. Nobody has to open it.
    • Advanced setup gives each of those choices a page of its own (advanced: true in plan.ts), next to the step it belongs with, and drops the Customize buttons that would repeat them.
  5. Modules come before the media and the look, so both are about what the server has. Your media also offers the module a kind of library needs, right on the kind.
  6. The words: no “port”, “P2P”, “NFO”, “metadata agent” or “API key” on a quick-setup page. A module is a module everywhere (not an add-on, not an extension). The server is named once on any page.
  7. TV mode: a part a remote does badly (colouring letters, importing a file, pasting keys) says so in the app’s usual words (pkg/sdui/data/tvlevels.json, docs/TV-MODE.md). Quick setup never needs one of them.

The steps#

#StepQuick setupCustomize (quick)Advanced pages
1WelcomeLanguage; Get started, Advanced setup, Restore from a backupDisc menus and sound tracks; time zoneLanguage and time zone
2Your accountSign-in name, password, server name, the legal tick; where data is kept (desktop installs that ask)Name people see; two-step sign-inServer name (colours, mark); People (profiles, required looks)
3ModulesModules, the folder browser ticked; InstallWhen a module has a new version(on the page)
4Your mediaKinds and folders; a module’s own page (Live TV, Games) when installedPosters and details beside files; one or separate librariesDiscs; Posters and details; Logos; Lyrics and artists; Karaoke voices; Streaming lists
5LookLayout, with its previewColours; TV guide (WittyTuner)Colours; TV guide
6FinishConnect a device; add a profile; this screen as a TV; Start watchingHome network; posters and details; logos; lyrics; karaoke; streaming listsHome network; Away from home

Defaults: language and disc tracks follow the browser; the computer’s time zone; details saved beside files; one library per kind; the folder browser ticked; module updates “tell me”; each look’s own theme; apps at home find the server by name; logos, online lyrics, artist pages, Demucs and streaming lists off.

Connect a device is the same component as Settings, Remote Access (components/ConnectDevice.tsx): one short code and a QR code, with the details further in. Setup doesn’t explain peer-to-peer or relays.

The server’s names for the steps#

GET /api/setup/state answers steps: welcome, account, modules, media, look, ready; a page is saved as the numbered step it’s part of. The names 0.8.0 to 0.8.2 used are still taken (oldSetupSteps in setup_state.go): language → welcome; server, name, storage, people → account; libraries, modulesetup, tv, games, discs → media; looks, theme, colours, wittytv, tvguide → look; network, remote, extras and its pages → ready. In the browser, an old page address, or an advanced page opened in quick setup, opens the page that holds its question now (currentStep in plan.ts).

What 0.8.3 removed, merged or reworded (#610)#

  • Merged: Language into Welcome; the server’s name into Your account; Libraries and the module pages into Your media; Layout, Colours and WittyTV into Look; People, Away from home and the five Extras pages into Finish (quick) or their own pages (advanced).
  • Removed from quick setup (still in advanced setup or Customize): Home network and its port editor, the disc start choice, required looks per profile, the peer-to-peer explanation, posters and keys, NFO conversion, logos, lyrics, karaoke, streaming lists.
  • Removed altogether: the dimmed “After you choose modules” and “Nothing to set up” steps, “Skip the extras”, the second “Skip for now” under the card, the per-page “change this later” lines, the Encrypted connections note, and Ready’s eight-line summary and its notes about library transcoding, TV channels and -listen.
  • Reworded: every page title and lead to one sentence; “Modules” lead; disc page; people page (“What people can change”); module updates.

Alpha software: it changes quickly and can fail or lose data. Keep backups, including your data folder, before you upgrade. No media comes with WittyMediaServer. Copy-protected discs, encrypted broadcasts and encrypted game files are refused with a plain message. Nothing here unlocks them.

This site has no cookies, trackers or analytics. Your choice of colours is kept in your own browser. How this site handles privacy. Change the colours.
