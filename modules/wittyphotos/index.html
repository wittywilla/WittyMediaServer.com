WittyPhotos

Photo libraries: what a photo says about itself, including RAW files, without decoding it.

RepositoryReleases

Reads what a photo says about itself without decoding it: when it was taken, with what camera and where, the picture’s size, and the JPEG preview inside a camera RAW file. It reads JPEG, TIFF, RAW, HEIC, AVIF, PNG and WebP files.

It is used by the photo library in WittyMediaServer, where the photo pages stay. Only a bounded part of each file is read, and a malformed file gives an error or empty fields.

What an update to it changes

All of it: reading EXIF, picture sizes, and the preview inside RAW files.

What stays with the server

The photo pages in the app.

To add it, go to Settings → Modules → Browse the store on your server.

Alpha software: it changes quickly and can fail or lose data. Keep backups, including your data folder, before you upgrade. No media comes with WittyMediaServer. Copy-protected discs, encrypted broadcasts and encrypted game files are refused with a plain message. Nothing here unlocks them.
