TWO PARTS, ONE SETUP
## The server, and the apps that reach it
WittyMediaServer runs on your own hardware and holds your library. You can use it from any web browser on your network. WittyMediaClient is the optional companion — native apps for the devices where a browser isn’t the right fit.
The server
### WittyMediaServer
Self-hosted software that stores and streams your films, shows, discs, music, books and games. It plays DVDs and Blu-rays with their real menus, in any browser on your home network.
Install it once on a machine you own. Every screen in your home reaches it through a web browser — no app required.

The companion apps
### WittyMediaClient
Native apps for TV boxes, phones, tablets and desktops that connect to a WittyMediaServer. They’re an app-based way to reach the server where a browser isn’t the right tool.
Optional, not required. The server’s full interface already runs in any web browser; the apps are for devices where a dedicated app feels better.
