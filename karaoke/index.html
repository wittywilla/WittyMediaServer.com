Karaoke and light shows

Turn any song in your library into karaoke, with the words big enough to read from the sofa and a light show behind them. It’s built in, it’s free, and it runs on your own server.

Nothing to set up

Open karaoke, and it gets ready by itself

  1. A quick version, in a few seconds. The server turns down what sits in the middle of the mix, which is where most singers are, and it plays straight away.
  2. A properly separated one, if you want it. Install Demucs, Spleeter or audio-separator on the server’s computer and it’s found by itself. The better version is made behind the quick one, and the sound changes over when it’s done.
  3. Next time, it’s there. What was made is kept, so the song opens straight into karaoke.

Already have the parts? Stems beside a song, such as Song.vocals.flac or a .stem.mp4, are always used first.

Honest about the quick version

Turning down the middle of the mix can’t take a singer out completely: echoes stay, and a snare or a solo in the middle goes down too. A song that’s close to mono has nothing to separate, and the screen says so. The separated version is the real thing.

On the karaoke screen

Everything a singer reaches for

Voice and Music

A slider for how loud the singer is, and one for the band. With a separated version there’s a slider for each part: drums, bass and the rest.

Voice off, or a guide

Take the singer out in one press, or keep them quietly in the background as a guide. Reset puts everything back.

Key

Moves the music up to six semitones either way, for the singer, without changing the speed.

Words on time

Nudge the words sooner or later a tenth of a second at a time. It stays with that song, for you.

Follow along, or Read

Lines that fill in as they’re sung with the next one waiting below, or every word at once to read at your own pace.

WittyVisualizer

Light shows for every song

Press Visualizer in the full-screen player, or V, and the music turns into light. Here are six of them, on the same song.

  • Bars, Waves and Drift are soft shapes in the artwork’s colours. They don’t flash.
  • The rest come after a notice about flashing, once, with the choice of a calm mode.
  • WittyVisualizer is a free module from the server’s store, and it also lights up WittyMediaPlayer.

Where the words come from

Your lyrics first, and online only if you say so

  1. A correction made on your server, if someone has fixed the words.
  2. A file beside the song: Song.lrc for timed words, or Song.txt.
  3. The song’s own tags, such as the lyrics in an MP3.
  4. Online, only if the owner turns it on: LRCLIB, free and with no account. It’s told the song’s artist, title, album and length, and nothing else.

Karaoke needs timed words. No times? Open Lyrics, then Fix, then Tap along, and press Space as each line starts. Your timing is kept on the server for everyone.
